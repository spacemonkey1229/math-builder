## The LEARNING GOAL CHECK

"Forces" test applied to every row: could a competent student produce this deliverable correctly
without ever performing this skill? If yes, the mapping is rejected.

- D1 requires converting every Appendix B line item from a US-dollar unit cost to a Jamaican-dollar
  total (the only currency-unit conversion in the case) and requires rounding the final Primary
  Sunk Investment total to the nearest thousand JA$ — which cannot be done correctly without
  identifying the thousands digit (place value).
- D2 requires taking JTL's stated annual percentage rate (10%) and the case's own quoted 25%
  Jamaican mortgage rate, finding the percent of a principal, and converting an annual dollar cost
  to a monthly one (a compound-unit conversion, dollars-per-year to dollars-per-month).
- D3 requires converting a weekly staff-hour figure into a monthly dollar cost (another
  compound-unit conversion) and computing the net dollar margin CIC keeps on a single one-hour
  visit after subtracting its own costs.
- D4 requires dividing a combined fixed-cost-plus-interest figure by a per-visit contribution
  margin to get a break-even visit count, and converting Appendix E's annual-rate scenarios into
  monthly visit counts to compare against that break-even, using percent-of-a-number twice
  (segment share × visits/year) before dividing by 12.
- D5 requires an original bar graph — not reproducing a case table, but consuming D4's own division
  results as fresh chart data.
