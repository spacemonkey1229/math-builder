## Rejections worth recording (so the next pass doesn't re-run these)

- **le.fbi.gov's own CODIS-NDIS statistics page** (the single most authoritative possible source for
  current NDIS size) returned HTTP 403 on every fetch attempt, including via `web.archive.org`
  (which this environment's WebFetch tool cannot reach at all). Do not keep retrying this exact URL
  with WebFetch — it appears to actively block this fetch method. A search-engine AI summary quoted
  numbers "from" this page, but those numbers were never independently verified against a fetched
  page and were excluded per case-research discipline (never cite a URL you did not fetch).
- **nature.com's hosted version of the 2026 Scientific Data NDIS-history paper** redirected to an
  authentication wall (`idp.nature.com/authorize`) and could not be fetched. The freely-available
  **arXiv preprint** of the same paper (arxiv.org/html/2511.11953v1) was used instead for
  cross-checking historical NDIS growth figures, though its specific numbers were not load-bearing
  in the final case (S2 carries the load).
- A clean, single, citable **per-locus** random match probability figure does not appear to exist
  in the freely accessible public literature — combined (multi-locus) RMPs are commonly published,
  but the per-locus breakdown depends on the specific alleles observed and the reference population,
  which varies case by case. The Freakonomics-reported 7.5% figure was the most concrete,
  attributable per-locus figure found after multiple search framings, and is disclosed in the case
  text itself as a representative average rather than an authoritative exact value.
