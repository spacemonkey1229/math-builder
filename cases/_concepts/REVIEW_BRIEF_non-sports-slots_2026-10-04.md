## Questions for each reviewer (per slot)
1. Is the story true/recognizable, and are all facts verified or honestly tagged UNVERIFIED?
2. Is the dilemma a real binary decision that survives the balance test (both sides defensible, no dominated option)? Several sheets say a framing is "constructed"; judge whether that is disclosed and acceptable.
3. Overlap with existing cases and domain diversity; sensitivity (accident site, human remains, living people).
4. Real free student-explorable data: confirmed, licensed, usable? Is any key figure unsourced?
5. Verdict GO / REVISE / DROP per slot (and say if the fallback is better), plus an overall APPROVE / BLOCK.
