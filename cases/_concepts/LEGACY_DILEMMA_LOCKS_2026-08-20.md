### player-of-the-year
**Locked as an analytics-methodology dilemma**, per feedback that a two-player pick was too
shallow — the binary is "which scoring methodology should the committee adopt," each option a
real, precisely-stated composite formula applied consistently across all 14 named finalists
(not two hand-picked players), computed end to end from the full stats spreadsheet. The split
between the two winners below was verified genuine, not forced — see the full audit trail.

**You are the AVCA Player Of The Year Committee. Two committee members have each proposed a
rigorous, formula-based scoring system — and they disagree. Which methodology should the
committee adopt?**

- **A.) Adopt the All-Purpose Production Index** — an unweighted, position-blind average z-score
  across Kills/Set, Aces/Set, Blocks/Set, Assists/Set, and Digs/Set (national mean/SD baseline;
  a category a player isn't nationally ranked in scores at league average, z=0, not zero).
  **Winner: Anna Stevenson (MB, Louisville)** — .427 hitting % and 1.35 blocks/set produce the
  highest average z-score of the 14 finalists (+0.526), narrowly ahead of Sydney Hilley (+0.517).
- **B.) Adopt the Role-Weighted × Team-Success Composite** — score each finalist only on the
  1–3 categories that define excellence at her position (pin hitters: kills/hit%/aces; middles:
  blocks/hit%/kills; setters: assists; libero: digs), then multiply by a team-success factor tied
  to her team's final RPI rank (up to +50% for a top-team boost, reflecting the AVCA committee's
  own stated practice of "weighing the quality of the teams" alongside individual stats).
  **Winner: Sydney Hilley (S, Wisconsin)** — her nationally-#1 11.95 assists/set is the single
  largest z-score in the entire dataset (+2.585); paired with Wisconsin's #2 RPI and their upset
  championship win over #1 Louisville, her composite score (+3.851) comfortably leads the field.

**Full 14-finalist data table, both formulas stated precisely, every player's computed score
under both methodologies, and the extraction/derivation method** are recorded in
`cases/_concepts/research_notes/player-of-the-year-analytics_2026-08-20.md` — hand this directly
to `case-write` as the "data necessary" appendix source when this case starts drafting. Source
data: the real 9-tab NCAA D-I volleyball stats spreadsheet and RPI rankings tab linked from the
case's own Appendix A, already present locally under `source/Y2/Session 5/Player Of The Year/`.

*Note on realism: both formulas are genuine, defensible statistical approaches (an unweighted
composite vs. a role-normalized, success-weighted composite) — this is intentionally a case about
**how you choose to measure**, not a rigged split. Methodology A's z=0-for-unranked convention and
Methodology B's specific team-success multiplier curve are modeling choices case-write should
treat as stated assumptions in Appendix F, open to the same ±50%-flex sensitivity test every other
case's assumptions get.*
