## Overview

"I'm sorry, Ann Morrison isn't available this year because of construction at the park," says the voice on the phone. Karen Dobson presses the phone tighter to her ear, already reaching for a notepad. "But the Simplot Sports Complex has open fields for those dates. It's bigger than Ann Morrison, but flat and open, and you can rent just the sections you need. I think it'd work well for your camp."

"Sure," Karen says, "that would be great. When do you need our reservation?"

"The sooner the better. One of the fields is already booked for a youth soccer tournament that same weekend, and even though it's a year out, it's a popular week."

Karen thanks the rep and hangs up, already pulling up the field list on her laptop. Nine sections, nine different prices, and a summer's worth of archers counting on her to get this right. She's done this every year since the camp started, but never with this many moving pieces at once.

---

## Background

Karen Dobson has coordinated Archery 360, LLC's summer camp for 4 years, growing it from 10 youth attendees its first year to over 50 last summer. She's confident next year's attendance will keep growing and wants to reserve space for **at least 100 youth archers**, ideally with room for up to **150**, for a **3-day camp**.

Archery 360 already owns enough bows, targets, and equipment for up to 200 attendees. Karen estimates it costs about **$26.30 per attendee** for a T-shirt and disposable equipment (arrows, replacement parts, and other consumables). Camp counselors are paid **$145.00 per counselor per day**. For safety, every archery range requires **at least 2 counselors**, and each range holds up to **12 youth participants** (SEE APPENDIX B). Archery 360's board has also set a real goal for every camp: keep the per-participant charge at or under **$110.00** whenever realistically possible.

The Simplot Sports Complex has nine rentable field sections, each a different size and price (SEE APPENDIX A). Karen has narrowed her choice to two combinations of sections. The planning figures she budgets from — how many attendees to plan for, and how a field's range count is treated — are fixed in **APPENDIX C**; use them, or your numbers won't match anyone else's. (SEE APPENDIX D for further reading on archery camps and the real venue.)

The vocabulary and the "how to…" skills this case will ask of you are listed in **Appendix E** — that is where Challenge 1 sends you.

## Appendix C: Modeling Assumptions

**Assumption 1 — Planning attendance.** Equipment and per-participant charges are calculated for **140 planned attendees** — within Karen's stated 100-150 target range, and a real, stated planning figure, not a guess to be re-derived.

**Assumption 2 — Full ranges, no partial refunds.** A field's listed range count (Appendix B) is fixed regardless of how many campers actually attend; unused range capacity is not a refundable cost.
