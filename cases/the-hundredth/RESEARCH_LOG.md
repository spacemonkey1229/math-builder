## Rejections worth recording
- **Olympedia's protest motive.** Olympedia's commentary says the Serbian protest cited Omega's sponsorship of Phelps; ABC News and Wikipedia say it alleged Cavic touched first without triggering the pad. The case attributes the allegation only to ABC's report and lists the discrepancy in Appendix A's notes and `excluded_sources`.
- **3 kg pad force** (Olympedia) and other force figures: secondary, inconsistent, not used.
- **"Serbia accepted the footage"**: appears only in a summary of NBC coverage with no named statement; kept cut, as the concept directed.
- **NBC "ten-thousandths" review claim**: uncorroborated; not used.
- **Concept said "SW 13.4.2 treats equal official times as ties"**: in the editions read, the equal-hundredths placing rule is SW 11.2; SW 13.4.2 is the combined-heats tie rule. The case cites both accurately.
- **Press-agency finish photo**: not used (copyright).
