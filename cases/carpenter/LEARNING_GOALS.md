## Forces test

For each mapping: *could a competent student produce this deliverable correctly without ever
performing this skill?*

- **D1** cannot be produced without computing rectangle areas from the floor plan's meter
  dimensions (geometry-how-to-find-the-area-of-a-rectangle), understanding what "area" measures
  (geometry-area), and converting m² to ft² (measurement-how-to-convert-an-amount-from-one-unit-to-another-unit).
- **D2** cannot be produced without applying the 2.6% delivery fee as a percent of a number
  (advanced-numbers-how-to-find-the-percent-of-a-number), working in two named currencies
  (measurement-currency), converting USD to CAD via the exchange rate
  (measurement-how-to-convert-an-amount-from-one-unit-to-another-unit), and applying a $/sq ft
  rate — a ratio — to each zone's square footage (measurement-ratio).
- **D3** cannot be produced without directly comparing each option's total against the CD$17,900
  cap (number-sense-how-to-compare-two-or-more-values).
- **D4** cannot be produced without plotting cost-vs-time data points as a bar or line graph
  (number-sense-bar-graph; a line graph reduces to plotting coordinate pairs of (year, cost),
  geometry-how-to-graph-a-coordinate-pair-on-the-coordinate-plane).
- **D5** cannot be produced without computing a percent difference between two totals
  (advanced-numbers-how-to-convert-numbers-to-percents, built on
  advanced-numbers-how-to-find-the-percent-of-a-number).
