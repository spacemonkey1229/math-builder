## Forces test

Every mapping above passes: *could a competent student produce this deliverable correctly
without ever performing this skill?* — No, in every case:

- D1 cannot be written without distinguishing the state's full county population from the
  working-roster population from the visit sample, and without computing two distinct percents.
- D2 cannot be written without actually running a simple-random first-stage draw (or explaining
  one already given) inside a stated multistage design, then computing a systematic interval and
  starting point per county — a student who only picked counties "that felt representative"
  has not done systematic sampling and the deliverable is visibly incomplete.
- D3 cannot be written without treating the 5 selected counties as sampling clusters and costing
  out a full census of each cluster's sessions, which requires addition and multiplication over
  the Appendix B session counts.
- D4 cannot be written without identifying which counties a convenience sample structurally
  excludes and without computing single-event and repeated-trial probabilities.
- D5 cannot be written without summing four cost categories across two different sets of five
  counties and computing rates from Appendix A/C figures.
- D6 cannot be produced without both graph types, each requiring the specific how-to skill.
