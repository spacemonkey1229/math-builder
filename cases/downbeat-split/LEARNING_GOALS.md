# Learning Goals — The Downbeat Split

Bidirectional deliverable ↔ skill matrix. Every Challenge-3 deliverable must map to a claimed
spine skill; every claimed skill must be forced by at least one deliverable.

| Deliverable | Skills it forces (spine ids) |
|---|---|
| D1 — Straight Cut total running time (mixed-number beat length × mixed-number seconds-per-beat, multiplied across every phrase repeat) | fractions-how-to-multiply-two-mixed-numbers |
| D2 — partial-phrase check (a fraction of the final phrase's mixed-number beat length) | fractions-how-to-multiply-a-fraction-and-a-mixed-number |
| D3 — Downbeat Split beat-subdivision calculation (fractional gap ÷ fractional subdivision unit) | fractions-how-to-divide-a-fraction-by-a-fraction |
| D4 — rehearsal read-through fit-check (mixed-number time remaining ÷ read-through length) | fractions-how-to-divide-fractions-and-mixed-numbers |
| D5 — final decision table (rehearsal minutes = mixed-number minutes-per-read-through × whole-number read-throughs, for each option) | fractions-how-to-multiply-two-mixed-numbers |

**"Forces" test applied to each row:**

- D1: the Straight Cut's total running time cannot be produced without multiplying the phrase's
  mixed-number beat length by the mixed-number seconds-per-beat and multiplying that product out
  across every repeat — this is the how-to skill itself, applied at the scale of the whole cue, not
  a shortcut around it.
- D2: locating the target cutoff inside the running time requires taking a genuine fractional
  portion (not a whole phrase) of a mixed-number beat length — a student who only ever multiplies
  two mixed numbers (as in D1) cannot produce this row; the mixed multiplicand here is scaled by a
  proper fraction, which is the distinct how-to skill named.
- D3: finding exactly how the beat must split to close the gap precisely is, by construction, one
  fraction divided by another fraction — there is no way to produce a subdivision that closes the
  gap exactly without performing this division, since "exactly" is the entire reason Option B
  exists instead of a second tempo nudge.
- D4: counting how many complete read-throughs fit inside the mixed-number rehearsal time left in
  the session is a division of a mixed number (or a fraction) by another fraction/mixed-number
  length — a student cannot get a whole-number count of complete read-throughs without dividing.
- D5: the rehearsal-minutes total in the decision table is not read off D4 directly — D4 finds how
  many read-throughs fit in the time *available*; D5 requires computing the time *actually needed*
  by multiplying the mixed-number minutes-per-read-through figure by the number of read-throughs
  it actually took, a fresh instance of the same mixed-number-by-mixed-number multiplication as D1,
  now applied to a different pair of quantities. It does not introduce a new spine skill, but it
  cannot be filled in without re-performing this one.
