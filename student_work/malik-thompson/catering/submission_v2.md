## Challenge 2 — Pitch (Beal wedding)

I pick the Beal wedding. Ms. Alvarez helped me work through the numbers appendix with her.

Wedding: 110 guests, revenue $3,186.00. Ingredients cost $1,700.38. Event fee $575, delivery
$13.20. Net profit = $3,186.00 − $1,700.38 − $575 − $13.20 = **$897.42**.

Corporate party: 100 guests, revenue $3,135.10. Ingredients cost $1,769.78. Same fees. Net profit
= $3,135.10 − $1,769.78 − $575 − $13.20 = **$777.12**.

The wedding makes about $120 more, so I pick the wedding.
