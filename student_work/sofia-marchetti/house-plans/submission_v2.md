## Challenge 2 — Pitch (Country Farmhouse)

I recommend the **Country Farmhouse**, but I have to be honest: this is much closer than I first
thought. Working the numbers from Appendix A (floor plans) and Appendix D (price sheet) at each
plan's most upgraded affordable tier:

- **Farmhouse:** build cost + amenity package = **$549,835.84**, which is **99.97%** of the
  $550,000 ceiling.
- **Ranch Estate:** build cost + amenity package = **$549,212.80**, which is **99.86%** of the
  ceiling.

The Ranch is actually about $623 cheaper — that's a margin of roughly 0.1%, basically a coin flip
on cost alone. I'm still picking the Farmhouse because its upgraded amenity package (pool, shop,
putting green, sport court, outdoor kitchen) fits a family that wants outdoor space more than the
Ranch's larger square footage does, and at a difference this small, the "which do we actually want
to live in" question matters more than the last few hundred dollars.

**Visual:** bar chart comparing both plans' total cost against the $550,000 ceiling line, showing
how close both bars sit to the top.

Strongest argument against my choice: $623 is $623, and a family right at their spending ceiling
might reasonably take the cheaper option on principle, even if the gap is tiny.
