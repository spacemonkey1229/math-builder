# House Plans — Sofia Marchetti

## Challenge 2 — Pitch (Country Farmhouse)

I think we should build the Country Farmhouse because it looks nicer and has a bigger porch and
the Ranch Estate is too plain and modern for a family home like this.

Cost-wise the Farmhouse is around $480,000 with upgrades which is under the $550,000 ceiling so it
works. The Ranch Estate is more expensive I think, maybe $510,000, so the Farmhouse saves more
money and is the better pick.

I looked at the floor plans in Appendix A and the price sheet in Appendix D to get these numbers.
