# SELF REVIEW

**Hero:** ______________  ·  **Case:** ______________  ·  **Date:** __________

Answer these three before you submit. Ten minutes, honestly. This does not set your verdict and
it is not scored — your guide reads it alongside your work, and so do you, next time.

> Per `RUBRIC.md`: neither review sets the verdict. Both are visible to the guide and to you.

---

**1. Which criterion is your work weakest on, and why?**

The seven: C1 research · C2 the pitch · C3 mathematical correctness · C4 required deliverables ·
C5 visual + appendix of work · C6 discussion · C7 framing the question.

*Naming your own weakest criterion accurately is worth more than being strong everywhere. If you
write "none of them," you have not answered the question.*

<br><br><br>

---

**2. What is the strongest argument against your position?**

Not the weakest one you can beat. The one that actually worries you.

*If you cannot write one, your pitch is probably a Not Yet on C2 — a position that hedges both
ways and a position with no live opposition fail the same test.*

<br><br><br>

---

**3. What did you have to look up?**

The thing you did not know before this case, and what you found. A false start counts. A
correction counts double.
