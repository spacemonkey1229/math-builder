# PEER REVIEW

**Reviewer:** ______________  ·  **Work by:** ______________
**Case:** ______________  ·  **Date:** __________

You are not grading this. You are rehearsing the Socratic discussion, and you are giving the
other Hero the one thing they cannot get on their own: a reader who has not been inside their
head for two hours.

> Per `RUBRIC.md`: peer review is **not** scoring. It is the cheapest way to raise C2 and C5
> across a cohort. A Hero who has been asked "where did you get lost?" once writes differently
> the next time.

---

**1. Could you follow the argument without re-reading? Where exactly did you get lost?**

Point at a sentence or a paragraph. "It was confusing" is not usable; "I lost you when you
switched from doses to litres without saying so" is.

<br><br><br>

---

**2. Does the visual actually support the position, or is it decoration?**

Cover up everything except the visual. Can you tell which side they are arguing, and why? If not,
say what the visual would have to show instead.

<br><br><br>

---

**3. What would you press them on in the discussion?**

Write the question you would actually ask out loud. One question, sharp.

<br><br><br>
